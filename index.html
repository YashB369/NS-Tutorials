About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Education, from First Steps to Final Degree

NS Tutorials is an educational institute dedicated to guiding students through every academic milestone — from Class 1 foundational learning to professional and post-graduate degrees. We combine personalized coaching, board exam support, and admission guidance under one roof, so families never have to look elsewhere.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which classes does NS Tutorials provide coaching for?
We provide coaching for students from Class 1st to 12th across all major subjects and boards.
Do you help with 10th private board exam forms?
Yes, we offer complete assistance for 10th private board exam form filling, eligibility checks and documentation.
Do you help with 12th private board exam forms?
Yes, we provide full support for 12th private board form filling across all streams, including admission guidance.
What graduation courses can I get admission guidance for?
We support admissions for B.Com, BMS, BAF, BBI, BBA, BA, B.Sc, B.Sc IT, B.Sc CS and B.Sc Data Science.
What post-graduation courses are available?
We assist with MA, MBA, MMS, M.Com, M.Sc IT, M.Sc CS and LLB admissions for both regular and distance modes.
Do you offer distance education programs?
Yes, we provide admission guidance for both regular and distance/correspondence education programs.
Is the coaching fee affordable?
Yes, NS Tutorials offers quality education at affordable fees designed to suit every family's budget. Contact us for the latest fee structure.
Do you guarantee results?
We follow a 100% result-oriented approach with regular tests, progress tracking and individual attention to help every student perform their best.
Do you provide career counseling?
Yes, our counselors guide students in choosing the right stream, course and career path based on their interests and goals.
Are healthcare programs like GNM and B.Sc Nursing covered?
Yes, we provide admission guidance for DMLT, AMDLT, OTT, X-Ray, GDA, GNM, B.Sc Nursing, BAMS, BHMS, MBBS, B.Pharmacy and D.Pharmacy.
How do I start the admission process?
Simply call, WhatsApp, or fill our inquiry form. Our team will guide you through counseling, documentation and admission confirmation.
